Lily Leap

Hop across the blooms and snap up every fly in the pond.

Walk
Hold the left and right pads.
Hop
Tap the big button. Hold it to jump higher.
Tongue
The pink button flicks your tongue at the nearest fly.
Bonus
Golden flies are worth 5×. Catch the shimmering dragonfly for a double jump. Purple bellflowers launch you sky-high.
Danger
Wasps patrol from pond 2. Don't fall in the water.

Keyboard: arrows or A/D, Space to hop, X to snap.

Tap anywhere to start the music.

Turn your phone sideways for a wider pond.

Play it like an app: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
